<p>Product photos form the core of any online catalog. AI transforms this fundamental image type:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Background Generation:</strong> Remove cluttered backgrounds instantly. Generate clean white backdrops for marketplace compliance or custom colors matching brand guidelines.</li>
<li><strong>Lighting Optimization:</strong> AI analyzes and enhances lighting automatically—shadow removal, highlight recovery, and exposure balancing.</li>
<li><strong>Angle Generation:</strong> Generate additional product views from limited source images. Upload a front-facing photo, receive side views and detail shots.</li>

</ul>

<h2>Best Practices for Success</h2>

<h3>Maintain Brand Consistency</h3>

<p>Define visual guidelines before generating images—preferred backgrounds, lighting styles, lifestyle themes, and environmental elements reinforcing brand values.</p>

<h3>Balance AI with Authenticity</h3>

<p>User-generated content and authentic behind-the-scenes imagery complement AI-generated photos. The combination builds trust while maintaining efficiency.</p>

<h3>Stay Transparent</h3>

<p>Consumer awareness of AI content grows. Maintain transparency about AI usage. Ensure images accurately represent actual products to avoid returns.</p>

<h3>Avoid Common Mistakes</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Over-Processing:</strong> Products appearing unrealistically perfect generate returns</li>
<li><strong>Ignoring Mobile:</strong> Most traffic is mobile—ensure images remain clear at smaller sizes</li>
<li><strong>Inconsistent Quality:</strong> Mixing high-quality AI with dated photography creates jarring experiences</li>
<li><strong>Neglecting Alt Text:</strong> AI images need proper SEO optimization</li>
</ul>
