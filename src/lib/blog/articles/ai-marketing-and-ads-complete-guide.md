<h2>Creating High-Converting AI Generated Ads</h2>

<h3>Step 1: Define Campaign Objectives</h3>

<p>Different objectives require different visual approaches:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Brand awareness:</strong> Memorable, distinctive visuals that communicate brand identity</li>
<li><strong>Consideration:</strong> Showcase product benefits and use cases</li>
